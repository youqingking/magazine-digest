# H1 Non-Goals

- no real push operations or lifecycle campaign operations
- no complex finance, revenue, or subscription dashboard
- no final release polish, app-store readiness, or broad UX polish pass
- no real growth closed loop completion such as live referral settlement or live promo redemption closure
- no multi-provider abstraction layer beyond the minimum needed for the chosen H1 payment path
- no rewrite of Stage G preview-only commercial or growth shells
- no conversion of smoke-only auth/harness entry points into customer-facing product features
