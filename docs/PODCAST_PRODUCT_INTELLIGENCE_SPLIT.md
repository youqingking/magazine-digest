# Podcast Product Intelligence Split

## Purpose

Step 08 freezes the split between shared architecture work and the future podcast product line.

From Step 08 onward, podcast product intelligence must branch into its own line of work.

## Shared Architecture Still Owns

Shared architecture continues to own only:

1. family-backed podcast projection
2. list surface
3. detail surface
4. discovery card surface
5. admin preview surface
6. retained extras
7. diagnostics

These remain architecture-facing and projection-facing outputs only.

## Podcast Product Line Must Split Out

The following are explicitly not part of Step 08 shared architecture:

1. `60s summary`
2. `3min structured brief`
3. `worth-listening signal`
4. `cross-episode compare`
5. `topic tracking`
6. `Chinese restructuring`
7. `reusable quote intelligence`
8. podcast flagship app search strategy
9. podcast flagship app feature-hub strategy
10. podcast flagship app subscription strategy

## Boundary Statement

The split point is now explicit:

- shared architecture ends at family-backed stable projection plus shared mobile/admin presentation surfaces
- podcast product line begins where editorial/product intelligence and flagship app strategy start

## Step 08 Guardrail

Step 08 must not:

- smuggle podcast intelligence into surface builders
- add product-intelligence payloads to manifests
- add ranking or listening heuristics into shared contracts
- use retained extras as a backdoor for podcast product intelligence

## Operating Rule

If future podcast work needs:

- summary generation
- worth-listening scoring
- cross-episode intelligence
- topic tracking
- quote extraction
- Chinese restructuring

that work must open a separate podcast product line thread and must not be folded into shared architecture Step 08 artifacts.
