# Domain Skeleton Migration

## Purpose

Step 05 freezes which selected domains get a minimal skeleton now, and which ones remain at pilot-only scope.

References:

- `docs/PILOT_REGISTRY_INTEGRATION.md`
- `docs/DOMAIN_PILOT_SELECTION.md`
- `docs/PODCAST_DOMAIN_POSITIONING.md`

## Why Step 05 Selects Three Domain Skeletons

Step 05 selects:

1. `magazine-domain`
2. `youtube-domain`
3. `podcast-domain`

Reason:

- magazine proves the direct-path domain skeleton on an already-established domain
- YouTube proves the direct-path video-summary skeleton without forcing transcript retro-fit
- podcast proves that a family-backed selected domain can exist above family contracts without turning family into product-domain truth

## Why Podcast Now Deserves A Selected Domain Skeleton

Podcast is no longer only a family pilot because it already has a coherent future product-domain seam above transcript family:

- show
- episode
- guest
- chapter
- feed metadata

Step 05 still freezes only the skeleton, not product behavior or migrated business code.

## Why SEC Stays A Family Pilot

SEC filing remains:

- a family-backed pilot
- a validation example for `official_structured_sources`

SEC does not become a product-domain skeleton in Step 05 because:

- it is still validating family path and route resolution
- it does not have a selected product-domain surface in this stage
- promoting it to a product domain now would overreach the stage boundary

## What Step 05 Does Not Migrate

Step 05 does not migrate:

- real domain business code
- product pages
- ingestion flows
- large-scale shared runtime adoption

Step 06 is the earliest stage that may consider actual selected-domain code skeleton migration work.
